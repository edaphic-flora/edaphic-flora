# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-11-15**

Edaphic Flora ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our soil data application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- **Email address** - Used for authentication and to associate your data submissions with your account
- **Authentication provider data** - If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google
- **Home zip code or state** (optional) - Used to show native status and wildlife for your area

### Soil Data You Submit
- Species and cultivar names
- Soil test values (pH, nutrients, texture, etc.)
- Location (a zip code or coordinates; a zip code is stored as the coordinates of its center)
- Sample dates and notes
- Outcome observations (thriving, struggling, etc.), sun exposure, and site moisture
- An optional link to an iNaturalist observation

### Automatically Collected
- Server logs (request timestamps, errors), kept short-term for operational debugging only; no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are loaded.
- An activity log of submissions, deletions, exports, and moderation actions

## How We Use Your Information

- **To provide the service** - Store and display your soil data
- **To build the community data** - Combine soil values by species, without your identity or location
- **To communicate** - Send important updates about the service (rarely)
- **To process uploads** - Soil report files are sent to AI services (Anthropic Claude) for data extraction. We do not store the file or its name

## Data Sharing and Visibility

### Your Submitted Soil Data
- **Is publicly visible** on species pages (soil values with the number of samples behind them, never your location, identity, sample dates, or notes) and in the downloadable community data file. When a species has only a few samples, the individual readings are shown
- **Is shared** under the [CC BY-NC 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) for non-commercial use
- **Your email is NOT publicly displayed** - only used internally for attribution

### We Do NOT
- Sell your personal information
- Share your email with third parties for marketing
- Use your data for purposes unrelated to Edaphic Flora

## Data Retention

- **Account data** - Retained while your account is active
- **Soil submissions** - Retained indefinitely as part of the community database
- **Uploaded files** - Processed for extraction and not stored. We keep a record that an extraction happened (when, and the file type)
- **Backups** - Private copies of the database are kept for about two months, so deleted data can remain in a backup for that long

## Your Rights

You may:
- **Access** your submitted data on your account page on edaphicflora.org, where you can also download it
- **Delete** your own submissions at any time. A deleted sample leaves the public pages and the data file at the next site refresh (weekly)
- **Delete your account** from your account page, which also deletes your samples, or by emailing edaphicflora@gmail.com. We typically action requests within 7 days

## Moderation

Admins may flag or hide individual submissions that appear erroneous, off-topic, or abusive. Repeated abuse or spam may result in an account being disabled (no new submissions, exports, or PDF extractions). Existing data from a disabled account is not auto-deleted; the data owner can still request deletion via email.

## Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- Secure database hosting with access controls
- No storage of passwords (handled by Firebase)

## Children's Privacy

Edaphic Flora is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be noted on the site or via email.
