Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-11

Edaphic Flora ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our soil data application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used for authentication and to associate your data submissions with your account.
  • Authentication provider data — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.

Soil Data You Submit

  • Species and cultivar names
  • Soil test values (pH, nutrients, texture, etc.)
  • Location coordinates (latitude / longitude)
  • Sample dates and notes
  • Outcome observations (thriving, struggling, etc.)

Automatically Collected

Server logs (request timestamps, errors) — kept short-term for operational debugging only. No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are loaded.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service — store and display your soil data, generate visualizations.
  • To improve the database — aggregate anonymized data for community analysis.
  • To communicate — send important updates about the service (rarely).
  • To process uploads — soil report files are sent to AI services (Anthropic Claude) for data extraction, then deleted.

Data Sharing and Visibility

Your submitted soil data is publicly visible in aggregate analyses and maps, and is shared under the CC BY-NC 4.0 license for non-commercial use. Your email is never publicly displayed — it's only used internally for attribution.

We do not sell your personal information, share your email with third parties for marketing, or use your data for purposes unrelated to Edaphic Flora.

Data Retention

  • Account data — retained while your account is active.
  • Soil submissions — retained indefinitely as part of the community database.
  • Uploaded files — processed for extraction and immediately deleted (not stored).

Your Rights

  • Access your submitted data through the app (My Data tab).
  • Edit or delete your own submissions at any time.
  • Request account deletion by emailing edaphicflora@gmail.com — we typically action requests within 7 days.

Moderation

Admins may flag or hide individual submissions that appear erroneous, off-topic, or abusive. Repeated abuse or spam may result in an account being disabled (no new submissions, exports, or PDF extractions). Existing data from a disabled account is not auto-deleted; the data owner can still request deletion via email.

Cookies

We use essential authentication cookies (via Firebase / Polished). No tracking or advertising cookies.

Data Security

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Secure database hosting with access controls.
  • No storage of passwords (handled by Firebase).

Children's Privacy

Edaphic Flora is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be noted in the app or via email.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email edaphicflora@gmail.com.
